ILLUSTRATIVE ENGAGEMENTS

Examples of practical work.

ILLUSTRATIVE ENGAGEMENT

Dutch GAAP to IFRS conversion

A Dutch company needs IFRS financial information for group reporting.

  • Assess accounting differences and calculate conversion adjustments
  • Prepare supporting reconciliations and document the accounting treatment
  • Deliver a GAAP difference assessment, adjustment schedules, technical memorandum and IFRS reporting support
ILLUSTRATIVE ENGAGEMENT

Monthly management reporting

A growing business needs better insight into financial performance.

  • Review monthly results against budget and analyse profitability and costs
  • Prepare cash flow forecasts and identify key performance drivers
  • Deliver a monthly reporting pack, variance analysis, cash flow forecast and performance commentary
