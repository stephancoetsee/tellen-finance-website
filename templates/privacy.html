Privacy information

This page explains how the current Tellen Finance pre-launch website handles information. The business is not yet accepting client enquiries or documents.

Information processed when you visit

The website serves static content through Cloudflare. Tellen Finance does not currently run a contact form, visitor accounts, marketing trackers or advertising analytics on these pages. Cloudflare may process technical connection information, including IP addresses and request logs, to deliver and protect the website.

Cookies and external services

We do not intentionally add marketing or analytics cookies. Cloudflare may use strictly necessary security-related technologies when needed to serve or protect the website. External websites linked from these pages have their own privacy practices.

Enquiries and client data

No enquiry form is currently active. Do not upload or send financial records or personal data to a website contact address until we announce the active business email and secure transfer process. Once enquiries are open, we will update this notice with the registered business identity, contact details, processing purposes, lawful bases, retention periods, recipients and applicable data-rights procedures.

Your privacy rights

Under applicable privacy law, individuals may have rights to access, rectify, erase or restrict processing of personal information and object to certain processing. The responsible business identity and direct contact channel will be published before client enquiry functionality is activated.
